💡 Physics Concepts:

Horizontal Velocity (Vx): Stays CONSTANT throughout flight because no horizontal forces act on it (ignoring air resistance).

Vertical Velocity (Vy): DECREASES over time due to gravity. At the peak, Vy = 0 (momentary stop), then becomes negative (falling).

Optimal Angle: 45° gives MAXIMUM DISTANCE because it perfectly balances horizontal and vertical components.

Equations: x = v₀·cos(θ)·t | y = v₀·sin(θ)·t - ½g·t²

Constructive Interference (0°): Peaks align with peaks, amplitudes ADD UP → LARGER wave

Real-World Examples: Noise-cancelling headphones, speaker systems, light diffraction patterns

💡 Physics Concepts:

Period (T = 2π√(L/g)): Time for one complete swing. Depends ONLY on length, not mass!

Simple Harmonic Motion: Repeating back-and-forth pattern. Displacement, velocity, and acceleration follow sinusoidal curves.

Energy Conservation: Potential energy at the top ↔ Kinetic energy at the bottom. Total energy CONSTANT!

Gravitational Force: F = GM·m/r² — Decreases with distance squared. Always points toward the star.
